# Third-party notices

This release contains project-authored prediction heads and analysis code. It
does not bundle the large third-party backbone weights or third-party source
repositories listed below. It also excludes raw residue-level prediction
exports generated by third-party comparators. Only aggregate metrics and
provenance hashes are retained.

- Review the upstream model card, repository, and license before use.
